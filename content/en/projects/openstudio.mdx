OpenStudio is my attempt at a complete answer to the next generation of video creation tools. The product is currently called **Mibu**.

It does not place a chat box beside a conventional editor. It combines an NLE core, an AI application center, a creative-agent workspace, and multi-platform publishing into one continuous production flow.

## From footage to publishing

- Rough cuts can be shaped directly from a transcript before exporting the final video.
- Nestable workflows turn repeated steps into automated production lines.
- A browser pool manages persistent sessions so publishing, RPA, and agents can safely reuse account state.
- Electron, FastAPI, React, and SQLite form a genuinely local-first desktop architecture.

I want it to become a creation space that feels both complete and open: the tools understand intent, while the creator keeps the final judgment.
